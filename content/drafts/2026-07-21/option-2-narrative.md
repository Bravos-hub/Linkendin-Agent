Ford's $30k pickup — From adapter anxiety to plug-and-play

For years, non-Tesla EVs at a Supercharger meant one thing: an adapter in the glovebox.

That small piece of plastic was a reminder that you were a guest on someone else's network.

Ford is now removing it from the equation.

The upcoming midsize electric pickup will ship with a native NACS port and a reported starting price around $30,000.

Not an adapter. Not a promise. A physical connector that talks to Tesla's cabinets directly.

It turns the largest DC fast-charging footprint in North America into infrastructure that just works for a mass-market truck.

The lesson? Sometimes the biggest charging upgrade isn't the battery; it's the plug.

If you were buying an EV pickup today, would native NACS access change your short list?
