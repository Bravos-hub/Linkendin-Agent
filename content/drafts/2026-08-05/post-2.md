Electric commercial vehicles — From pilot fleets to production volume

Passenger EVs dominate the headlines.

Yet the strongest economics of electrification have always lived in commercial fleets — high daily mileage, predictable routes, depot-based charging.

July's numbers make that visible.

BYD sold 8,139 commercial electric vehicles in July, up 149.2% year over year and 31.7% over June.

Buses and trucks, not sedans and crossovers.

Fleet buyers purchase on total cost of ownership, not brand loyalty. When the math works, adoption does not trickle — it jumps.

The lesson? The loudest segment of a market is rarely the fastest-growing one.

Is commercial electrification on your fleet's roadmap yet, or is it still a passenger-car conversation in your market?
