Xpeng in Australia — from distributor collapse to factory control

Xpeng's Australian distributor, True EV, went into administration after less than two years.

Only the G6 is currently on sale, yet an estimated 2,000 units have already been delivered.

The lesson from BYD's earlier distributor troubles was already clear: direct factory control can be more reliable than an unknown local partner.

Xpeng Motors Australia Pty Ltd is now taking over importation, distribution, authorised dealers, parts supply, servicing, and customer support.

The company has unveiled a five-year expansion plan in Melbourne and expects to launch five new models before the end of the year, including the flagship X9 and the affordable Mona M03.

Globally, Xpeng has sold more than 1 million vehicles.

The lesson? Overseas expansion is not just about product — it is about who owns the customer relationship after the sale.

For markets with Chinese EV entrants: does direct factory control matter more than price or range?
