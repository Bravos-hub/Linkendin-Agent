Managed charging is moving from private garages to public parking lots.

PURA selected AmpUp's Adapt & Earn program to scale statewide — the only one of four Cycle 1 pilots to advance.

The 18-month pilot with Eversource enrolled 1,000 ports across 200 locations.

Participants allowed short charging pauses during peak hours for wallet credits inside the AmpUp app.

During a heatwave, the program shifted load off-peak when wholesale prices spiked more than 3,600%, cutting relief cost to roughly one-third of the displaced peak supply. Driver engagement was strong: 91% reported no inconvenience. Site-host enrollment was 100%.

The platform delivers incentives natively, so drivers at workplaces, multifamily buildings, and campuses do not need a utility account, vehicle telematics, or extra hardware. The utility gets a flexible load; the driver gets a credit; the host gets a revenue stream.

The lesson? Public chargers can behave like grid assets when the control layer and incentive flow are built into the charging software.
