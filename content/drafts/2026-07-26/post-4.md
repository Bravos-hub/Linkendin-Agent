The Data Center Backlash — From Zoning Fights to the Real Question

The war over AI is being fought at local zoning boards.

More than a dozen US data center projects have been blocked or delayed by local opposition, and New York has signed a one-year halt on its largest data centers — the first moratorium of its kind in the nation.

A March Gallup poll found 71% of American adults oppose AI data center construction in their area, with environmental concerns the most common reason.

Psychologist Adam Mastroianni argues the fight over where data centers go is really a proxy for a harder question: what role AI should play in our lives.

For the energy sector, the distinction matters. Interconnection queues, transmission upgrades, and tariff structures are being shaped by a debate that is only superficially about megawatts.

The lesson? The loudest infrastructure debates are rarely about infrastructure.

Are we planning grids for the load AI actually creates, or for the controversy it attracts?
