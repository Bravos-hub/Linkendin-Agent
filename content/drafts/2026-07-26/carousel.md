## Slide 2
**The machine nobody sees**
Terminal tractors shuffle trailers and containers inside ports and yards.
Kalmar Ottawa invented the category in 1943 — and its diesel T2 became a fixture.
design_notes: Cutaway diagram of a terminal tractor coupling to a trailer, minimal line-art style, navy/teal palette.

## Slide 3
**A duty cycle built for batteries**
Low speeds. Constant stop-and-go. Never leaves the yard.
Full torque from 0 rpm — exactly where diesel is at its worst.
design_notes: Circular route diagram of a yard with a dotted loop path and a depot charge point marked with an amber bolt icon.

## Slide 4
**The hardware matches the job**
Continuous-duty electric drive motor.
Up to 454 kW (roughly 610 hp) of peak output.

## Slide 5
**Battery sized to the shift**
Modular lithium-ion pack: 105, 140, or 210 kWh.
The spec question is energy per shift, not range.

**Now working in Canada**
WTC Group is deploying T2 EVs with support from Transport Canada's iMHZEV incentive program.
Gross combination weight rating: up to 195,000 lbs (~88.5 tonnes).
